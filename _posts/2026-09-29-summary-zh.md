## [Qwen3-VL 8B 在复杂文档提取任务中与云端大模型对比测评](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1wsbqni/qwen3vl_8b_on_a_laptop_vs_opus_55_sonnet_5_gpt56/) ⭐️ 7.0/10
